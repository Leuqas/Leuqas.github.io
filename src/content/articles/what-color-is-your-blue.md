Oh man, I love blue.

It sucks to think: what if the red you see is what I see as blue?

That doesn’t really make sense, right? Here’s the thing: this idea came from a thought I had when I was seven. What if people see colors differently, but we’re taught to associate them with the same names?

From the outside, everything would still seem consistent.

Take a leaf. You might see it as the same color I see in the sky. But since we were both taught that a leaf—no matter what our eyes physically see—is green, we’d still call it green.

Everything would line up. Every object that shares a leaf’s color would make you think, *Hey, that’s green.* Because as far as either of us knows, green looks like green.

Is that why people have different favorite colors?

I hope so. I truly believe there’s a color above all the others. I believe the blue I absolutely adore is the same blue everyone else looks up to—just with a different name.
