> &emsp;“The ultimate goal would be to eventually be placed in a *superposition*, where using that quote would feel right and wrong at the same time, **yet I'd collapse the wave function and say it anyway**.”

&emsp;I have a habit of getting obsessed with things. Pag ako na curious, I can spend hours trying to understand how it works, what I can make with it, or why the idea keeps coming back to me. Shet, Schrödinger’s cat has been one of those topics for the longest time. It’s such an absolute nerd of a topic: a cat in a box, a quantum event, and a question that somehow makes “knowing” feel complicated. I found it fascinating, and then, of course, I kept thinking about it.

&emsp;That kind of focus shows up in other parts of my life, too. For the longest time, I have been programming. The website you're probably reading this is on a blog platform, created by me. I play guitar, endulging myself both on the satisfaction of *being* able to play, and at the same time, the process of learning *how* to play itself. I golf—a sport where every single shot should be engineered because no two balls land on the same spot. I’ve crocheted tiny stuffed toys. I made a leather wallet that I used for about a damn year, lost, and eventually found again in my golf bag, already molded. I built a guitar pedal circuit from scratch, and even whittled balsa wood, para lang sa hayop na gift. The projects are all different, but they come from the same place: I like looking at something and thinking, *gago, I can make that.*

&emsp;I don’t always finish every idea, and not every project turns out the way I imagined. But I like figuring things out with my hands. A piece of leather, a circuit, a bit of wood: each starts as material with possibilities, then gradually becomes something specific.

&emsp;For a while, I didn’t think of that as especially important. They were just hobbies, things I did when I had time. Then there were times when being alone felt heavier than I expected.

&emsp;I tell people this, too: **find yourself again**. In my case, that can mean remembering:

&emsp;***“Oh shit, I have hobbies nga pala.”***

I have so much life in me that it’s funny how I oftentimes forget it when there’s so much life in front of me.

*haha, back to Schrödinger’s pussy.*

&emsp;Basically the proposition of Schrödinger's cat goes something like this. There exists a cat, consealed within a box, and that box holds a mechanism that detects whether an atom decays, or not decays. If the atom decays, which is roughly a 50/50 chance, the mechanism releaeses poison, killing the cat. If it does not decay, the cat lives. Heres where it gets weird. Schrodinger states that an atom behaves differently from your usual object, that it has a quantum superposition of two states. That it can both be *decayed, and not decayed* all at the exact same time, *until* it is observed. 

&emsp;Now, since the condition of the cat would be dependent on the state of whether a superpositioned atom's state, the cat with this applied logic would be dead and alive at the same time, until someone shows up and opens that box to observe it. The thought experiment asks what happens when that description gets connected to something as large and familiar as a cat.

&emsp;Schrödinger came up with it in 1935 to point out how strange that connection seemed. He wasn’t saying a regular cat is simply alive and dead in the everyday sense until somebody opens the box. And “observation” here doesn’t mean a person peeking inside. It’s a question about quantum measurement, which is a whole technical rabbit hole on its own. But then again, I'm not here to write an academic paper lmao

Time check: May 23, 2026. Peak of my writing, and somehow romantic period even without a desire to be in a relationship—awit, **just romanticising the thought of romance**. 

&emsp;Then pumasok sa utak ko mga *banat*. Usually, when someone gives a person flowers, the line is something like, “You’re prettier than the flowers.” Which is fine, but I wondered how perfect it would be to combine that familiar banat with a topic ive been nerding about.

Somehow, after obsessing over Schrödinger’s cat for so long, my brain came up with: 
## **“Schrodingers Rose: ‘The prettiest flowers— until you're around’”**. 
*I know, very normal use of my free time;* taking this absolute bull of a topic and making shit out of it. bull+shit, *bullshit*. 

*Oh diba, mas may bitaw sa traditional na banat. How they ARE the prettiest flowers, until [person's] around. perfectly reflecting the concept of **quantum mechanics**.*

The ultimate goal would be to eventually be placed in a *superposition*, where using that quote would feel right and wrong at the same time, **yet I'd collapse the wave function and say it anyway**.
