> &emsp;“I have so much life in me that its funny how I oftentimes forget it when theres so much life in front of me.”

&emsp;I have a habit of getting obsessed with things. Pag ako na curious, I can spend hours trying to understand how it works, what I can make with it, or why the idea keeps coming back to me. Shet, Schrödinger’s cat has been one of those topics for the longest time. It’s such an absolute nerd of a topic: a cat in a box, a quantum event, and a question that somehow makes “knowing” feel complicated. I found it fascinating, and then, of course, I kept thinking about it.

&emsp;That kind of focus shows up in other parts of my life, too. I’ve crocheted tiny stuffed toys. I made a leather wallet that I used for about a year, lost, and eventually found again in my golf bag, already molded. I’ve made other things out of leather, built a guitar pedal circuit from scratch, and even whittled balsa wood, para lang sa hayop na gift. The projects are all different, but they come from the same place: I like looking at something and thinking, *gago, I can make that.*

&emsp;I don’t always finish every idea, and not every project turns out the way I imagined. But I like figuring things out with my hands. A piece of leather, a circuit, a bit of wood: each starts as material with possibilities, then gradually becomes something specific.

&emsp;For a while, I didn’t think of that as especially important. They were just hobbies, things I did when I had time. Then there were times when being alone felt heavier than I expected.

&emsp;I tell people this, too: **find yourself again**. In my case, that can mean remembering:

> &emsp;***“Oh shit, I have hobbies nga pala.”***

&emsp;That’s the whole premise of this essay.

&emsp;I have so much life in me that it’s funny how I oftentimes forget it when there’s so much life in front of me.

&emsp;Dude, I golf, lift consistently, play guitar in a band, and do freelance programming for real businesses—not to mention the side projects I obsess over for a few weeks. I’m proud of the career and life I’m building. I used to think of hobbies as separate from the serious parts of life. Now I see the same curiosity running through them: I like learning how things work, then trying to make something that works.

&emsp;*haha, back to Schrödinger’s pussy.*

&emsp;Schrödinger’s cat is an absolute nerd’s dream: a cat in a sealed box, a radioactive atom, a detector, and a mechanism that may or may not release poison. If the atom decays, the detector triggers the mechanism; if it doesn’t, the cat survives. **Quantum theory describes the atom as being in a superposition of possible states**, and the thought experiment asks what happens when that description gets connected to something as large and familiar as a cat.

&emsp;Schrödinger came up with it in 1935 to point out how strange that connection seemed. He wasn’t saying a regular cat is simply alive and dead in the everyday sense until somebody opens the box. And “observation” here doesn’t mean a person peeking inside. It’s a question about quantum measurement, which is a whole technical rabbit hole on its own. Sobrang fascinating, but also very easy to oversimplify.

Time check: June 15, 2026. Peak of my writing, and somehow romantic period even without a desire to be in a relationship—awit, **just romanticising the thought of romance**. 

&emsp;Then pumasok sa utak ko mga *banat*. Usually, when someone gives a person flowers, the line is something like, “You’re prettier than the flowers.” Which is fine, but I wondered how perfect it would be to combine that familiar banat with a topic ive been nerding about.

&emsp; Somehow, after obsessing over Schrödinger’s cat for so long, my brain came up with: **“Schrodingers Rose: ‘The prettiest flowers; until you're around’”**. *I know, very normal use of my free time;* taking this absolute bull of a topic and making shit out of it. bull+shit, *bullshit*. 

Oh diba, mas may bitaw sa traditional na banat. How it IS the prettiest flowers, until you're [someone(s)] around. perfectly reflecting the concept of quantum mechanics


The dream would be to eventually get the chance to use that quote.
